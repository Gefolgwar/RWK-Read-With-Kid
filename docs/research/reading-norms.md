# Reading norms for primary-school children: Ukraine and Germany

Researched 2026-11-16 for issue #39 (parent spec #35). Only figures found in a primary source are listed.
Where none was found, this file says so.

The app's info dialog (`ReadingNormsDialog` in `LibraryScreens.kt`, on the main screen and in the history)
does not show these figures as they are. By the owner's decision it shows one rounded set of guide values by grade,
without sources and without splitting by country, marked as approximate and not binding:

| Grade | Words per minute | Words per day |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 25–40 | 300–500 |
| 2 | 50–90 | 1,000–1,500 |
| 3 | 75–110 | 1,500–2,000 |
| 4 | 90–140 | 2,000–2,500 |

Derivation: the speed spans the Ukrainian and German figures below. Grade 1 has no official figure in either
country, and grade 3 none in Germany; those values are estimates between the sourced ones. Words per day is the
speed times 10–15 minutes of reading (grade 1), 15–20 minutes (grade 2) or 20 minutes (grades 3–4, as in the
German Leseband). Neither country has an official figure for words per day.

## Ukraine

### Reading speed

**Source:** MON order No. 1222 of 21.08.2013, "Про затвердження орієнтовних вимог оцінювання навчальних
досягнень учнів із базових дисциплін у системі загальної середньої освіти", as amended by MON order
No. 1009 of 19.08.2016. Section "Літературне читання … 2-4 класи", table "Кількісні показники темпу читання
вголос (для розвитку темпу читання)".

- Order and annex text: https://osvita.ua/legislation/Ser_osv/36975/ (annex: https://osvita.ua/doc/files/news/369/36975/dodatki.doc)
- Annex of order 1009 on the MON site: https://mon.gov.ua/static-objects/mon/sites/1/zagalna%20serednya/pochatkova/vymogy-do-kontorlyu-ta-otsinyuvannya.pdf
  (Cloudflare blocked automated download; the figures were read from the osvita.ua copy of the annex)

| Grade | 1st semester | 2nd semester |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 35–45 words/min | 50–60 words/min |
| 3 | 65–70 words/min | 75–80 words/min |
| 4 | 80–85 words/min | 90–95 words/min |

Status of these figures, quoted from the same annex: "Окремо темп читання із застосуванням кількісних показників
учителем не перевіряється. Подані показники є орієнтиром для розвитку темпу читання, а не перевірки та оцінювання."
(Teachers do not test the reading tempo separately with these numbers; they are a guide for developing the
tempo, not for testing or grading.)

The current assessment recommendations for the New Ukrainian School (MON order No. 813 of 13.07.2021,
https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/go/v0813729-21) describe reading aloud only qualitatively ("у відповідному темпі")
and give no words-per-minute figures. The 2016 table above is therefore the latest official set of figures found.

Not sourced:
- **Grade 1**: the table starts at grade 2. Figures for grade 1 circulating online (e.g. "30–40 words/min")
  come from school reports and blogs, not from MON documents.
- **Silent reading**: blogs give figures for grades 3–4, but they were not found in the MON annex text.

### Daily amount

No MON document with a recommended daily reading time or number of words for primary pupils was found
(searched MON orders 1222/1009 and 813). Left out.

## Germany

### Reading speed

**Source:** Staatsinstitut für Schulqualität und Bildungsforschung (ISB) Bayern, LehrplanPLUS Grundschule,
Servicematerial "Erläuterungen Lesegeschwindigkeit": https://www.lehrplanplus.bayern.de/serviceinformation/l49225

The values concern reading aloud and are guideline values (Richtwerte), in words per minute (WpM):

| Grade | Average readers | Very good readers |
|---|---|---|
| End of grade 2 | "etwa 80-90 WpM richtig vor" | "etwa 115 WpM" |
| End of grade 4 | "etwa 130-140 WpM richtig vor" | "etwa 165 WpM" |

The ISB page takes these from: Rosebrock, C. / Gold, A. / Nix, D. / Rieckmann, C. (2011). *Leseflüssigkeit fördern:
Lautleseverfahren für die Primar- und Sekundarstufe.* Seelze: Klett-Kallmeyer, p. 54 ff.

Not sourced: grades 1 and 3 (the source gives only ends of grades 2 and 4). There is no nationwide (KMK) figure;
the KMK Bildungsstandards describe fluent reading without a number.

### Daily amount

Several states make reading time a fixed part of every school day ("Leseband"), 20 minutes a day:

- **Brandenburg** (Bildungsserver Berlin-Brandenburg, project of the Ministerium für Bildung, Jugend und Sport):
  "verbindliche Lesezeiten (20 Minuten täglich) fachübergreifend im Stundenplan zu verankern"
  — https://bildungsserver.berlin-brandenburg.de/themen/sprachbildung/projekt-leseband-brandenburg
- **Schleswig-Holstein** (Ministry of Education press release, 08.02.2024): "Das Projekt Leseband enthält eine
  verbindliche, tägliche Lesezeit von 20 Minuten unabhängig vom Fach"
  — https://www.schleswig-holstein.de/DE/landesregierung/ministerien-behoerden/III/Presse/PI/2024/Februar/20240208_Leseband

This is reading time at school, not a homework recommendation. No official figure for daily reading at home was found.
Mecklenburg-Vorpommern also reports a daily 20-minute Leseband at all public primary schools, but its press page
could not be retrieved during this research, so it is not cited.
